# First steps

GTNA follows GregTech's tiers. This is roughly the order in which its content shows up. A modpack can change every recipe, so your pack's quest book has the final say.

| Stage | What GTNA adds | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Steam | large steam machines, hydraulic parts, the Large Steam Solar Boiler, wireless steam, primitive machines with no energy, the Steam Elevator | [Steam age](../gameplay/machines/steam-age.md), [Wireless steam network](../gameplay/systems/wireless-steam-network.md) |
| LV–HV | Nexus energy network, Industrial Slaughterhouse, Accelerate Hatches, more processing multiblocks | [Nexus energy network](../gameplay/systems/nexus-energy-network.md), [Industrial Slaughterhouse](../gameplay/machines/industrial-slaughterhouse.md) |
| EV–IV | Electric Void Miner and Void Fluid Drilling Rig, Pattern Buffers at scale, auxiliary modules | [Void mining](../gameplay/systems/void-mining.md), [ME Pattern Buffers](../gameplay/systems/pattern-buffers.md) |
| LuV–UV | mega machines, Thread and Overclock Hatches, data and computation | [Hatches and parts](../gameplay/parts/index.md) |
| UHV–UXV | Space Elevator, Nano Forge, Dyson Sphere, Eye of Harmony, original multiblocks | [Systems](../gameplay/systems/index.md) |
| UIV–MAX | Bose-Einstein Condensate, Forge of the Gods, Stellar Containment Chamber | [Forge of the Gods](../gameplay/systems/forge-of-gods.md) |

## Habits that help

- **Read the controller tooltip.** Every GTNA machine says what it does, its parallel and its quirks; the last line names the mod it was ported from.
- **Look at the structure in JEI/EMI** before building. Hover a position to see which blocks and hatches fit there.
- **Let the terminal build it.** The [Nexus Structure Terminal](../gameplay/systems/modules.md#nexus-structure-terminal) places big structures for you.
- **Check the catalog** when you meet a new machine: [168 pages](../gameplay/machines/catalog/index.md) with block lists.
- **Use the wireless networks early.** Steam and EU without pipes or cables save a lot of building.
