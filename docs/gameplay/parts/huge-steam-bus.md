# Huge Steam Buses

Steam item buses with **54 slots**, for steam multiblocks that process many stacks at once, such as the Large Steam machines.

| Bus | Slots |
|---|---:|
| Huge Steam Input Bus | 54 |
| Huge Steam Output Bus | 54 |

They go wherever a steam item bus goes. Two related parts:

- **Infinite Steam Input Bus:** steam multiblocks read its items without consuming them (a restricted part, see [Hatches and parts](index.md#restricted-parts)).
- **Output Boost Steam Output Bus:** multiplies the item outputs of a steam multiblock (also restricted).
