# Original multiblocks

Machines designed for GTNA, with no equivalent in another mod. Each controller has its own screen with a **field manual** (in English, Portuguese and Chinese), and each page below has the formulas, tables computed from the code and the current screens.

| Machine | Tier | What it does | Only it makes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Kinetic Orbital Lance](originals/kinetic-orbital-lance.md) | UEV | fires projectiles at moons and asteroids from a capacitor bank and recovers the fragments in capsules | Primordial Tholin (Arrokoth), Nexium (Nexus-1) |
| [Dimensional Rift Gate](originals/dimensional-rift-gate.md) | UIV | opens rifts to seeded dimensions; tune frequency, phase and amplitude until the rift holds | one exclusive material per biome: Prismatium, Chronomercury, Forgecore, Vivispore, Abyssal Antimatter, Precursor Residue, Void Ash |
| [Antimatter Collider Ring](originals/antimatter-collider-ring.md) | UIV | collides counter-rotating beams for exotic particles and stores antiprotons in Penning traps | antiproton cells, Higgs and top particles |
| [Stellar Containment Chamber](originals/stellar-containment-chamber.md) | MAX | holds a neutron star in field bands and sends the energy of its spin to the Nexus network | Magnetar Crystals, Degenerate Core Matter |

The four feed each other and the endgame: their products go into the Forge of the Gods, the Eye of Harmony field generators and the antimatter fuel rods.

## Dependencies

- Lance → **Nexium** → Collider (tier-4 dipoles) and **Magnetarium** (the slug that breaks Nexus-1).
- Rift Gate → **Forgecore** plates → Stellar Chamber (holds the magnetar field).
- Stellar Chamber → **Magnetar Crystal** → Magnetarium → Lance.
- Collider → **X-Nexus** → Rift Gate (anchors past depth 40) and the Forge of the Gods.
