## Description

Turns foam back into dust. Magic, but with vacuum. Dries flotation ore foams back into dusts, Red Mud and Water, or runs the Dehydrator.

- Machine Type: Vacuum Drying, Dehydrator
- Dehydrator: parallel 2x every 900K of coils
- Select Dehydrator mode at the controller
- Coils must meet the recipe temperature
- Vacuum Drying always runs serially
