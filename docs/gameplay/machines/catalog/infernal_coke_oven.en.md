## Description

Hell's kitchen, but for coal. A coke oven that gets faster the longer it runs, and grows stronger every 10 minutes.

- Parallel: starts at 8, up to 256
- Steam: 6,400 L/s base, +1,600 L/s per stage
- Speed bonus decays while idle
