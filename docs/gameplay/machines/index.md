# Machines

GTNA registers **170 multiblocks**. Every one has a page in the [catalog](catalog/index.md), generated from the mod code: a render of the structure, the in-game description, the recipe types and the block list.

| Category | Count | Start with |
|---|---:|---|
| Steam and primitive | 66 | [Steam age](steam-age.md) |
| Electric processing | 63 | the [catalog](catalog/index.md#processing) and the [Industrial Slaughterhouse](industrial-slaughterhouse.md) |
| Power generation | 10 | [Nexus energy network](../systems/nexus-energy-network.md) |
| Energy, AE2 and computation | 6 | [ME Pattern Buffers](../systems/pattern-buffers.md) |
| Endgame systems | 20 | [Forge of the Gods](../systems/forge-of-gods.md), [Eye of Harmony](../systems/eye-of-harmony.md) |
| GTNA originals | 4 | [Original multiblocks](originals.md) |
| Developer tools | 1 | the Duration Tester, for testing hatches |

## Where they come from

Most machines are ports, credited in game on the last tooltip line and on each catalog page:

- **GT: Not Leisure** (54): the large steam machines and many steam-age structures.
- **GregTech Odyssey** (82) and **GregTech Leisure** (9): most electric and endgame multiblocks, including the Eye of Harmony and the Dyson Sphere.
- **GT: New Horizons** (14): Forge of the Gods, Bose-Einstein Condensate, Nano Forge, Space Elevator.
- **Twist Space Technology:** Eye of Wood.
- **GTNA originals:** the four [original multiblocks](originals.md) and several systems.

Licences and permissions are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/GTInfinityAscension/GregTech-Nexus-Addon/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Building big structures

- JEI/EMI shows every structure layer by layer.
- The [Nexus Structure Terminal](../systems/modules.md#nexus-structure-terminal) builds a structure for you, picks coil and casing tiers, and can take blocks from an ME network.
- Some machines accept [auxiliary modules](../systems/modules.md) that unlock more hatches or recipe types.
