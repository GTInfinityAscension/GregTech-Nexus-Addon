# Hydraulic components

GTNA's steam age has its own crafting components, the hydraulic parts. They play the role of electric motors, pistons and pumps in the recipes of GTNA's steam multiblocks, the wireless steam hatches and the Steam Elevator.

| Component | Used in |
|---|---|
| Hydraulic Motor | the other hydraulic parts; Stone Superheater; Large Steam Ore Washer, Mixer, Centrifuge and Thermal Centrifuge |
| Hydraulic Piston | Hydraulic Arm; Large Steam Hammer, Compressor, Extractor, Lathe, Extruder and Sifter |
| Hydraulic Pump | Hydraulic Steam Receiver; Large Steam Extractor, Ore Washer and Bath; Primitive Distillation Tower; Steam Cracker |
| Hydraulic Arm | Steam Manufacturer; Large Steam Cutting Machine; Steam Elevator greenhouse and bee modules |
| Hydraulic Conveyor | Large Steam Alloy Smelter, Bath, Cutting Machine, Bending Machine, Extruder and Wiremill |
| Hydraulic Regulator | wireless steam hatches; Cactus Wonder; tier II and III Steam Elevator modules |
| Hydraulic Vapor Generator | tier III Steam Elevator modules; Compact Hyper Pressure Reactor |
| Hydraulic Steam Jet Spewer | Hydraulic Vapor Generator; Steam Elevator and its tier I modules; Steam Item Vault |
| Hydraulic Steam Receiver | Steam Elevator modules; Void Miner SteamGate Aged |
| Precision Steam Component | most Large Steam multiblocks (Crusher, Furnace, Hammer, Compressor, Extractor, Ore Washer…) |

## Two ways to make them

- **Crafting table:** the first parts have shaped recipes made of Clay Compound plates, iron rods and rotors, bronze gears and tiny bronze fluid pipes.
- **Hydraulic Manufacturing:** the same parts in the [Steam Manufacturer](../machines/catalog/steam_manufacturer.md). It runs on steam (16 mB/t per parallel) and processes many at once. The steel wireless steam hatches and the Steam Elevator are made here too.

JEI/EMI lists every recipe under *Hydraulic Manufacturing*.
