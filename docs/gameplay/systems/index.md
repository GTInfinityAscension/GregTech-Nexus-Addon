# Systems

The mechanics that tie GTNA's machines together, roughly in the order you meet them.

| System | Era | In short |
|---|---|---|
| [Wireless steam network](wireless-steam-network.md) | Steam | one steam pool per player, no pipes |
| [Hydraulic components](hydraulic-system.md) | Steam | the crafting parts of the steam age |
| [Modules and Structure Terminal](modules.md) | all | optional extra structures, and a tool that builds structures for you |
| [Nexus energy network](nexus-energy-network.md) | LV onward | wireless EU with a Flux Matrix as storage |
| [ME Pattern Buffers](pattern-buffers.md) | with AE2 | patterns, inputs and modes in one multiblock part |
| [Void mining](void-mining.md) | Steam, EV and later | ores and fluids without digging |
| [Research engine](research-engine.md) | pack-defined | a research tree that modpacks fill with their own nodes |
| [Item effects](item-effects.md) | — | animated icons of the endgame materials |

## Endgame

| System | Tier | In short |
|---|---|---|
| [Bose-Einstein Condensate](bec.md) | UIV | a network of condensate and nanites for the hardest crafts |
| [Space Elevator](space-elevator.md) | UHV | orbital modules: assembly, asteroid mining, gas collection |
| [Nano Forge](nano-forge.md) | — | builds nanites; four tiers |
| [Dyson Sphere](dyson-sphere.md) | — | a swarm around the star, turned into laser power |
| [Eye of Harmony](eye-of-harmony.md) | UXV | mines a whole planet per run |
| [Forge of the Gods](forge-of-gods.md) | UIV | an artificial star feeding up to 16 processing modules |
