    Materials¶

    GTNA registers 187 materials as GTCEu materials in the gtna namespace. Their items, blocks and fluids work like any GTCEu material, and JEI/EMI shows every form and recipe.

    Steam-age materials¶

    Material Made of Notes
    Stronze 1 Bronze + 2 Steel blast furnace at 1,123 K; casings and pipes of the steam machines
    Breel 2 Bronze + 1 Steel no blast furnace: mixed by hand and smelted, as in GT: Not Leisure
    Clay Compound Flint + Clay + Stone plates and gears of the first hydraulic parts
    Compressed Steam steam solid steam for steam-age recipes

    The supercritical steams that boost the Void Miner SteamGate Aged are GTNA fluids too.

    Endgame materials (SpaceTime, Universium, MagMatter, White and Black Dwarf Matter…) have animated icons; see Item effects.
